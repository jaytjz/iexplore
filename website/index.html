The Problem

Why AI Image Detection Matters

With the rapid advancement of generative AI technologies, distinguishing between real and AI-generated images has become increasingly challenging. This poses significant risks in areas such as:

  • Misinformation and fake news
  • Digital authentication and verification
  • Copyright and intellectual property
  • Social media credibility
